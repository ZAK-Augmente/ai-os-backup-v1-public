AI OS Backup V1

A personal utility for keeping encrypted backups in the owner’s Google Drive.

The utility packages selected files and encrypts them on the backup host before upload. It checks the uploaded copy and keeps recovery points so the owner can restore files after a loss.

Google authorization is limited to the per-file drive.file scope. This site describes the utility and its data practices. The utility is operated by its owner for personal use.
